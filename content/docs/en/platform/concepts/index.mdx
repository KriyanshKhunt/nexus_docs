# Concepts Overview

Before integrating APIs or SDKs, it is important to understand **how Nexus Signal is architected** and how it manages notifications at scale.

Nexus is built on a decoupled, event-driven pattern designed to give you absolute control over templates, channels, and delivery costs.

## Architectural Pillars

Nexus Signal is designed around **four engineering principles**:

### 1. Asynchronous Event-Driven Processing

To keep your backend fast, ingestion is separated from delivery execution. The REST API validates and enqueues event payloads in under **10 milliseconds**, responding immediately with `202 Accepted`. Heavy operations (compiling templates, calling third-party carrier gateways) run asynchronously in dedicated worker threads.

### 2. Bring Your Own Provider (BYOP)

Nexus does not resell carrier bandwidth. You connect your own SendGrid, Twilio, Resend, or AWS SNS API credentials. You pay the carriers directly at cost, avoiding the per-message markups charged by legacy notification engines.

### 3. Multi-Tenant Enterprise Isolation

Nexus provides first-class support for B2B multi-tenancy. You can scope preferences, email layout styling, in-app bells, and even routing rules to individual customer organizations (tenants).

### 4. End-to-End Observability

Every notification lifecycle state (`Ingested` → `Queued` → `Processing` → `Dispatched` → `Delivered` → `Read` or `Failed`) is tracked with precise timestamp telemetry, making it easy to audit delivery failures or provider latencies.

## Unified Product Messaging Model

Beyond standard event triggers, Nexus unifies multiple application messaging channels under a single control plane:

- **[Objects & Subscriptions](/docs/platform/features/objects-subscriptions)**: Scopes notification delivery to non-user entities (like workspaces, projects, Slack channels, or document boards) and handles fan-out alerts to watching subscribers automatically.
- **[Broadcasts & Campaigns](/docs/platform/features/broadcasts)**: Allows team members to dispatch marketing or system announcements to audiences directly from the dashboard.
- **[In-App Guides & Banners](/docs/platform/features/guides)**: Delivers targeted banners, toast messages, and modal prompts to in-product users via real-time WebSocket feeds.
