title: Cost Analytics
description: Track provider spend per channel, workflow, and provider with budget alerts.

Know exactly what your notifications cost. Nexus tracks carrier-level spend across all messaging channels by correlating successful deliveries with your custom provider price sheets.

## How it works

1. Define your provider message unit rates in **Settings → Integrations → Provider Costs** (e.g., SendGrid at `$0.001` per email, Twilio at `$0.0075` per SMS).
2. When a notification resolves to a success status, Nexus references the active provider key from the execution's `responseMeta` field.
3. The spent amount is calculated as `count * costPerMessage` and added to the organization's monthly billing cycle total.

## Setup

### 1. Set provider costs

Navigate to **Integrations → Provider Costs** in the dashboard and specify custom unit costs in USD for each active integration.

### 2. Set monthly budget cap

Navigate to **Settings → General** and specify a monthly `costBudgetUsd` cap (e.g., `1000` USD) to trigger notifications and alerts when spending exceeds limits.

## Dashboard API

Retrieve the active cost breakdown from the analytics controller:

```http
GET /v1/analytics/overview/cost?environment=PRODUCTION
Authorization: Bearer <jwt_dashboard_token>
```

## Budget alerts

When monthly cost exceeds the configured `costBudgetUsd` budget cap:

- An organization badge turns to `Exceeded` in the dashboard overview.
- An email warning notification is sent automatically to all organization admins once per billing cycle.
- Future triggers are **not** blocked — notifications continue to send, but alerts keep admins informed of run rate trends.

## Optimization tips

Combine cost analytics with engagement conversions (`seen`, `interacted` rates) to identify high-cost, low-engagement workflows. If a workflow runs up high Twilio costs but has low action rates, consider:

- Introducing [presence suppression](/docs/platform/features/cost-reduction) to skip SMS for active in-app users.
- Swapping the channel fallback order to prefer email or push before SMS.
- Introducing a [digest window](/docs/platform/features/digest-throttle) to consolidate multiple alerts into a single batch email.
